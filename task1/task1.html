Black Myth: Wukong

About

Black Myth: Wukong is a 2024 action role-playing game developed and published by Game Science. The player assumes the role of the Destined One, a staff-wielding monkey, who embarks on a journey to recover six relics corresponding to Sun Wukong's six senses.

Review

Black Myth: Wukong is a visually stunning action RPG praised for its breathtaking graphics, deep Chinese mythology, and exceptional boss battles, making it a must-play for Journey to the West fans. However, some critics find its combat shallow, level design inconsistent with frequent invisible walls, and overall story less compelling than its presentation, though its challenging, dodge-focused fights and exploration are generally rewarding.

Detroit: Become Human

About

Detroit: Become Human is a narrative-driven game set in a future where androids gain sentience and fight for equal rights. Players control three androids—detective Connor, housekeeper Kara, and revolutionary Markus—whose choices dramatically impact the branching story and the fate of both humans and machines in Detroit.

Review

Detroit: Become Human is a visually stunning narrative adventure praised for its impactful, branching story where player choices genuinely matter, leading to massive replayability and diverse endings, though some found its handling of complex themes like civil rights heavy-handed and its gameplay overly simplistic
